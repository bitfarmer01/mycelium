Northwind Robotics On Call (sample data)

This page is fictional sample data for testing Mycelium. Northwind Robotics (sample data) is not a real company.

Rotation

On call rotations run Monday to Monday. The primary on call engineer carries the pager for one week.

Escalation

A page to the primary on call is acknowledged within 10 minutes or it escalates to the secondary on call.

If the secondary does not acknowledge within 5 minutes, the page goes to the on call manager.

Severity

Severity 1 means a customer facing outage. Page the primary immediately.

Severity 3 means no customer impact. Handle it on the next business day.
