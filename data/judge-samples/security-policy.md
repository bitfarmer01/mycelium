# Northwind Robotics Security Policy (sample data)

Northwind Robotics (sample data) is a fictional company. This policy is fictional sample data for testing Mycelium.

Revision 2, effective 2025-09-15.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report a suspected security vulnerability to security@northwind.example within 24 hours of discovery.

Do not test against production customer data.

## Accounts

Production access requires a hardware security key, not a phone app.

Break glass accounts are reviewed every quarter.

## Logs and retention

Northwind keeps security audit logs for 180 days.

Audit logs older than 180 days are deleted.

## Devices

Every laptop must have full disk encryption turned on before its first use.
